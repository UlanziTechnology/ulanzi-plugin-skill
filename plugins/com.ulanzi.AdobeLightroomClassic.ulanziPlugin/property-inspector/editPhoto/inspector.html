Tips
  1. Rotate to adjust. Press to reset the current parameter. Hold and rotate to switch parameters and show a Lightroom toast.
  2. After first installing the plugin, restart Lightroom Classic and confirm that Ulanzi is enabled in File > Plug-in Manager.
  3. If Lightroom Classic is open but disconnected, reload the Ulanzi plugin in File > Plug-in Manager.
